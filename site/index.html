What you get

One chat room. A whole workshop.

Team

Bot team

Each bot has its own model, role, memory, and budget. A coordinator dispatches; specialists deliver.

Space

Group chat

Message the room or @-mention a specific bot. Handoffs between bots are visible, not hidden.

Flow

Task dispatch

Bots delegate work to each other. You watch the chain, not just the final answer.

Trust

Approval gate

Every write operation asks you first. Allow once, deny, or save it as a rule.

Cost

Budgets

Token, cost, turn, and wall-time limits per bot and per room — with auto-stop when they blow.

Proof

Audit trail

Every tool call, approval, and budget stop is recorded. Filterable, honest, local.

Governance

Serious software for untamed agents.

Multi-bot AI is powerful and easy to over-trust. Codar Bot treats every bot as a talented contractor: welcome in the room, watched at the toolbox.

Approvals

Nothing writes without you

File edits and shell commands pause for an Allow / Deny decision — with optional rules for the routine stuff.

Budgets

Spending you set in advance

Caps per bot and per room in tokens, money, turns, or wall time. A blown budget pauses the room — no surprise bills.

Audit

A record you can point at

An append-only, filterable log of every tool call and decision — stored on your disk, owned by you.
